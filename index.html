Where to?

Explore More

Uttar Pradesh
Uttar Pradesh, in northern India, is renowned for its historical landmarks like the Taj Mahal and rich cultural heritage.
Bihar
Bihar, located in eastern India, is a land of ancient history and spirituality, home to sites like Bodh Gaya and Nalanda University.
Andhra Pradesh
Andhra Pradesh, located in southern India, is known for its rich cultural heritage, vibrant traditions, and historic temples.
Karnataka
Karnataka, in southern India, is renowned for its diverse landscapes, historic monuments, and thriving IT hub in Bengaluru.
Rajasthan
Rajasthan, located in northwestern India, is celebrated for its majestic forts, palaces, and desert landscapes. Known as the "Land of Kings".
Telangana
Telangana boasts a rich cultural tapestry, blending traditional South Indian customs with Persian influences. The state's heritage includes iconic historical sites like the Charminar and Golconda Fort
